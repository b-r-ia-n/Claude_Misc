# Rest

Four collage essays on rest in the manner of Eliot Weinberger, plus the research behind them. Terra's "Rest: Bodies and Postures" (2026-10-31) is a companion piece; it isn't in this repo.

| # | Essay | Angle | Model |
|---|-------|-------|-------|
| 01 | [Forty Less One](01-sabbaths.md) | sabbaths, sacred and ritual rest | Opus |
| 02 | [The Sleeping Ones](02-sleeping-animals.md) | how animals and plants sleep | Opus |
| 03 | [The Sixth Hour](03-idleness.md) | idleness, leisure, the politics of rest | Opus |
| 04 | [The Fermata](04-stillness.md) | pauses, stillness, the long sleep | Fable |

[facts.md](facts.md) holds all 215 gathered facts in one file, each with source, status and tags. The per-essay originals are in `facts/`.
